Plain-language policy · August 28, 2026

Your board stays yours.

Continuity Board stores project names, notes, uploaded reference images, prop lists, shot cards, and license information on your device. We do not receive your board contents and there is no account or analytics tracker.

Local storage

Your board is held in IndexedDB. A Studio license token and its last verification result are held in localStorage. Clearing site data removes them from this device. JSON backup is available so you can keep or move your work.

Billing and license checks

If you buy Studio, the checkout opens on Sociobot’s billing service. Sociobot and Dodo, the merchant of record, process purchase details under their own policies. This app sends only your license token to Sociobot to check whether it is valid, at most once per day. Payment card data never enters this app.

Uploaded references

Images are user-created references and remain in this browser. Print/PDF and JSON exports are created on your device. Exported files include the attribution text you supply; you decide where those files go.

Contact

Questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
